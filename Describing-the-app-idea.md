# Poop Diary: App Idea

Poop Diary is a simple mobile app for people who want to track their digestive health. Users can quickly record food, bowel movements, symptoms, water, exercise and sleep.

The app shows all entries in a daily diary and provides basic trends for 7, 14 or 30 days. Users can view, edit and delete their records. Poop Diary helps users understand their own data, but it is not a diagnostic app.

```text
Today
[ Food ] [ Bowel ] [ Symptoms ] [ Water ]

Today's diary
08:00  Food     Oatmeal + banana
08:45  Bowel    Type 1
```
